Quick answers

How do I join the team?

Applications open each August. Any first- to third-year engineering student can apply; no racing experience is required, only curiosity.

Can my company sponsor the car?

Yes. Sponsors support us with funding, materials or equipment, and every sponsor is credited on the car page. Use the form above or the sponsorship button below.

Do you offer workshop visits?

We host open workshop days twice a semester for school groups and industry partners. Send us a message to book a slot.

Where are you based?

Inside the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka.
